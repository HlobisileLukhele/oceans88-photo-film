Questions

Asked most often.

If yours isn't here, WhatsApp us. We reply within 24 hours.

Do you travel outside Durban?

Yes. We shoot across KwaZulu-Natal as standard. Travel beyond 50 km from Durban is quoted per kilometre, and further afield we quote travel and accommodation up front.

Who brings the props and styling?

For food and beverages we bring surfaces, backdrops and basic props. A food or prop stylist can be added to any package. You supply the product and, where possible, the person who makes it.

Can we use the photos and films anywhere?

Yes. Every package includes a perpetual licence for your own marketing: web, social, print, menus and in-store. Paid media buys and billboards are included too. Resale to third parties is not.

How many revisions do we get on video?

Two rounds of changes are included on every film. We share a private link where you can leave comments on the exact second you want changed.

What do you need to book a date?

A signed quote and a 50% deposit. The balance is due on delivery of final files. Dates move once without charge with 72 hours' notice.

Do we need model releases?

For staff, guests and talent who appear clearly in the work, yes. We bring release forms and handle them on the day, in line with POPIA.
